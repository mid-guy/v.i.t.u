# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text at that link applies to all project spaces: issues, pull
requests, discussions, and any other channel used for this project.

## In short

- Be respectful and considerate. Disagree about code, not about people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, or publishing of
  other people's private information.
- Assume good intent, and accept constructive feedback gracefully.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks this code, contact the
maintainer, [@mid-guy](https://github.com/mid-guy), privately through the
contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may
temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behaviour they consider
inappropriate, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor
Covenant.
